# Editorial photography

Used for the home page hero, category cards, closing section and sign-in panels.
These are REPRESENTATIVE images of a category - never presented as a specific
vehicle in the fleet. Vehicle cards and detail pages always show the fleet's own
uploaded photos, so the car a customer books is the car they saw.
